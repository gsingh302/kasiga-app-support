Loop Bloom privacy policy

Effective July 12, 2026

Loop Bloom does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The game works offline and includes no advertising, analytics, account system, tracking technology, or third-party gameplay SDK.

Information stored on the device

Loop Bloom stores game progress locally on the player's device. This may include level progress and best results for Daily Bloom and Speed Seeds, such as move counts and completion times. This information is used only to provide game features on that device. It is not transmitted to Kasiga Innovations or any third party.

Local game information may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Because Loop Bloom does not operate an account or cloud-save service, Kasiga Innovations cannot view, recover, or transfer local progress.

Sharing

Loop Bloom opens the operating system's share sheet only after the player selects Share Solve. The player chooses the destination and may edit or cancel the message. Loop Bloom does not receive the selected destination or information about resulting social activity.

Haptic feedback

The game may request on-device haptic feedback for actions such as rotating a tile or completing a puzzle. Haptic feedback is processed entirely on the device and does not collect or transmit information.

Children's privacy

Loop Bloom does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other player. The game does not provide chat, messaging, user-generated content, purchases, or advertising.

Third-party services

Loop Bloom does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, social-login, or tracking services. If the player voluntarily shares a result, the privacy practices of the destination selected in the operating system share sheet apply to that destination.

Changes to this policy

If Loop Bloom's privacy practices change, this policy and the app's privacy information in the applicable app store will be updated. Material changes will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact and support

For support, feedback, or privacy questions, email kasigainnovations@gmail.com.
