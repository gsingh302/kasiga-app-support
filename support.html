Game support

Need help with Kinetic Halo, Chroma Surge, or Nova Chain? Email kasigainnovations@gmail.com.

Include the game name, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Quick troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen the game.
  2. Restart the iPhone and confirm the current game version is installed.
  3. Check that sufficient device storage is available.
  4. If the problem continues, send the steps that reproduce it and a screenshot when possible.

Progress and accounts

The games require no account and store progress locally on the device. Removing an app can also remove its local save data.
